Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our products and services

Orders & Purchasing

Placing an order is simple! Browse our products, add items to your cart, proceed to checkout, fill in your delivery details, select a payment method, and confirm your order. You'll receive a confirmation email once your order is placed.

You can modify or cancel your order within 2 hours of placing it. Go to "My Orders" in your account, select the order, and click "Cancel Order" or "Modify Order". After this period, please contact our customer support for assistance.

You can track your order by visiting the Track Order page or through your account's "My Orders" section. You'll also receive tracking updates via email and SMS at each stage of delivery.

There is no minimum order value at Akku.GG. You can purchase any product regardless of price. However, free shipping is available on orders above NPR 5,000 within Kathmandu Valley.

Shipping & Delivery

Delivery charges vary by location:

  • Kathmandu Valley: NPR 100 (Free on orders above NPR 5,000)
  • Outside Valley (Major Cities): NPR 200
  • Remote Areas: NPR 300-500 depending on location

Delivery times depend on your location:

  • Kathmandu Valley: 1-2 business days (same-day delivery available for select products)
  • Major Cities: 3-5 business days
  • Remote Areas: 5-10 business days

Currently, we only deliver within Nepal. We're working on expanding our services internationally. Stay tuned for updates!

Our delivery partner will attempt to contact you via phone. If you're unavailable, they will make 2 more delivery attempts. You can also reschedule delivery through your order tracking page or contact customer support.

Payment & Pricing

We accept multiple payment methods:

  • Cash on Delivery (COD)
  • eSewa
  • Khalti
  • Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard)
  • Net Banking
  • Mobile Banking

Yes, COD is available for all orders. However, COD may not be available for high-value items (above NPR 100,000) or in certain remote areas due to security reasons.

Absolutely! We use industry-standard SSL encryption for all transactions. We don't store your card details on our servers. All payments are processed through secure, PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways.

Yes, an invoice is included with every order. You can also download a digital invoice from your account's "My Orders" section after the order is delivered.

Returns & Refunds

We offer a 30-day return policy for most products. Items must be unused, in original packaging, with all accessories and tags intact. Some products like software, opened electronics, and personal care items may have restrictions.

To return a product, go to "My Orders", select the order, click "Return Item", choose the reason for return, and submit. Our team will arrange pickup within 2-3 business days. You can also visit our store with the product and invoice.

Refunds are processed within 7-10 business days after we receive and inspect the returned item. The amount will be credited to your original payment method. For COD orders, you can choose bank transfer or store credit.

Yes, exchanges are available for the same product (different size/color) or a different product of equal or higher value. Contact our customer support to arrange an exchange.

Account & Profile

Click on "Login" in the top right corner, then select "Create Account". Fill in your details including name, email, phone number, and password. You'll receive a verification email to activate your account.

Click on "Login", then select "Forgot Password". Enter your registered email address, and we'll send you a password reset link. Follow the instructions in the email to create a new password.

Yes, go to "My Account" > "Profile Settings" to update your email or phone number. You'll need to verify the new email/phone number through an OTP sent to your device.

To delete your account, contact our customer support with your request. Please note that account deletion is permanent and you'll lose access to your order history, wishlist, and saved addresses.
