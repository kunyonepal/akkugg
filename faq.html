Orders, delivery, payment, returns and your account

Orders

Add products to your cart and open Checkout. Enter your name and mobile number (email is optional), choose delivery or store pickup and a payment method, then press "Place Order". You get an order number on the screen, and an email if you gave your address. We then call you to confirm the order.

You can cancel an unpaid order yourself on its order page until we have confirmed it. After that, or if you have already paid, contact us before the order is sent and we cancel it at no cost. Any payment is refunded in full within 7 days.

Delivery

  • Inside Kathmandu Valley: delivery charge told to you before dispatch
  • Outside Kathmandu Valley: delivery charge told to you before dispatch
  • Store Pickup: free (Putalisadak, Kathmandu, Nepal)

Full details are in the delivery policy.

The usual time for each area is shown at checkout and in the delivery policy. It is an estimate; we tell you if there is a delay, and if we cannot deliver in the time we told you, you can cancel for a full refund.

Payment

  • Cash on Delivery: Pay when your order arrives
  • eSewa
  • Khalti
  • ConnectIPS
  • Fonepay

Online payments are made on the payment service's own page (for example eSewa or Khalti), so we never see your wallet PIN or bank password. The whole website uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

We are not registered for VAT, so no VAT is added. Every order gets an invoice. The delivery charge is shown separately at checkout.

Once your order is confirmed, open it from Track order and use the invoice link.

Returns & warranty

Yes, ask within 7 days of delivery. Wrong, damaged or not-as-described items are returned at our cost. For a change of mind, the item must be unused and complete in its box. See returns and refunds.

Open your order and use "Ask for a return or a warranty repair". Pick the item and the reason, and tell us what happened. You get a reference number and we contact you about the next step.

Within 7 days after we accept the return, back to the way you paid where possible.

Each product page shows its warranty. You can check an item by its serial number on the warranty check page. See warranty information.
