Warranty Information

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Each product page shows the warranty for that product. Warranty starts on the day you receive the product. You can check an item by its serial number on the warranty check page.

1. Warranty periods

  • Shop warranty: the number of months shown on the product page, counted from delivery. We handle these claims ourselves.
  • Manufacturer's warranty: some products also come with the maker's own warranty, described on the product page or on the warranty card in the box. Some makers handle repairs through their own service centres; we help you contact them.
  • After delivery, your order page lists the warranty end date for each item.

2. How to make a claim

  • Open your order from Track order and use "Ask for a return or a warranty repair".
  • Choose the item, describe the fault and add the serial number if it has one.
  • You get a reference number (it starts with RMA). Keep the product's box and accessories if you can.
  • You can also call us or bring the product to our store at Putalisadak, Kathmandu, Nepal with the order number.

3. What happens next

  • We contact you to collect the product or arrange a visit to the store, and we check it.
  • If the fault is covered, we repair it, replace it, exchange it or refund it at no charge. For some brands the repair is done by the maker's service centre, which can take longer; we tell you the expected time.
  • If we find no fault, or the problem is not covered, we tell you before doing anything and only go ahead with a paid repair if you agree.
  • We keep you updated by SMS and email, and the progress shows on your order page.

4. What is not covered

  • Physical or liquid damage, drops, cracks and burns.
  • Damage from misuse, power surges, or repairs and changes made by someone else.
  • Products with a removed or altered serial number or warranty seal.
  • Normal wear, such as scratches or worn keycaps, and parts that are used up, such as batteries losing capacity over time, unless they fail early.
  • Software problems, viruses and data loss. Please back up your data before handing in a device.

This does not affect your rights under Nepal's consumer protection law.

5. Common questions

I lost my invoice. Can I still claim?

Yes. We keep a record of every order. Give us the order number, or the phone number used for the order, and we will find it.

Is there a fee for a warranty claim?

No, covered repairs and replacements are free. If a fault is not covered, we tell you the cost first.

Can I claim if I received the product as a gift?

Yes. Bring the product and the order number or the buyer's phone number.

Not happy with how a claim was handled?

Use the complaints page. We give you a decision within 15 days.
