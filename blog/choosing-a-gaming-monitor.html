Choosing a Gaming Monitor

21 Nov 2026
2 min read
Akku.GG

Your monitor decides what you actually see from your graphics card. Three things matter most: resolution, refresh rate and panel type.

Resolution

  • 1080p (Full HD): easiest to run, great for fast competitive games, best at 24 inches.
  • 1440p (QHD): the sweet spot for most gamers at 27 inches: noticeably sharper, still reachable at high frame rates with a mid-range or better card.
  • 4K (UHD): very sharp at 27 to 32 inches, but needs a high-end graphics card for smooth gaming.

Refresh rate

A 60Hz screen shows at most 60 frames a second. A 144Hz or 165Hz screen makes movement look much smoother, and 240Hz or more helps in fast shooters if your PC can produce that many frames. Match the refresh rate to the frame rates your graphics card can reach in your games.

Panel type

  • IPS: accurate colours and wide viewing angles. A good all-rounder.
  • VA: deeper blacks and higher contrast, good for darker games and films. Some models show smearing in fast scenes.
  • OLED: perfect blacks and very fast response, at a higher price.

Also check

  • Adaptive sync (G-SYNC Compatible or FreeSync) removes tearing.
  • Ports: DisplayPort, or HDMI 2.1 for consoles at high refresh rates.
  • Stand: height and tilt adjustment makes long sessions more comfortable.
A good pairing: a mid-range graphics card with a 27-inch 1440p 144Hz+ IPS monitor.
