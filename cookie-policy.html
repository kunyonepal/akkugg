Cookie Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This page lists what akku.gg keeps in your browser and why. The shop does not use advertising or analytics cookies at the moment.

1. Kept by the shop on your device

These are kept in your browser's own storage (not cookies). They stay on your device and are not sent to anyone else, except where noted:

  • Cart, wishlist, compare list, PC build and recently viewed products, so they are still there when you come back.
  • Recent searches and your choice of grid or list view.
  • Currency choice (NPR or USD).
  • Orders placed from this browser, with the private link to each one, so you can open and track them.
  • Your delivery details, only if you tick "Save my details on this device" at checkout.
  • How you reached us (for example an ad, or a link from another website). This is sent with an order or quotation request so we know which of our ads work.
  • Your answer to the cookie notice, and which promotion popups you have already seen.

2. Cookies set by the shop

These are only set when you use the part of the site that needs them, and the site cannot work without them:

  • akku_staff and akku_dev: keep shop staff signed in to the admin pages (up to 12 hours) and recognise their devices, so the owner is warned about sign-ins from new ones. Customers never get them.

3. Advertising and analytics cookies

The shop does not load advertising or analytics tags at the moment. If that changes, this section will list them, and they will only run if you agree.

4. Your settings

To remove everything this website has saved in this browser (cart, wishlist, saved details, the list of your orders on this device and your cookie choice), use the button below. Your account and orders on our side are not affected.
