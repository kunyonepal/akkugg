Returns & Refunds

Last updated: 24 November 2026

You can ask to return an item within 7 days of delivery. If the item is wrong, damaged on arrival or not as described, we pay for the return and you get a replacement or a full refund.

1. Return window

Ask for a return within 7 days of the day the order was delivered (or collected from the store). The order page shows the delivery date.

2. How to return

  • Open your order from Track order and use "Ask for a return or a warranty repair".
  • Pick the item, the reason ("Changed my mind", "Wrong item sent", "Damaged on arrival" or "Not as described") and tell us what happened. Add the serial number if the item has one.
  • You get a reference number. We contact you to arrange pickup or a visit to our store, and we keep you updated by SMS and email as the return moves on.
  • You can also call us or visit the store with the order number.

3. Condition of the item

For a change-of-mind return, the item must be unused, undamaged and complete, in its original box with all accessories, manuals, free gifts and warranty cards. If the item has been used or something is missing, we may refuse the return or tell you before we take off a fair amount for it.

For a wrong, damaged or faulty item, return it as you received it, with the box if you still have it.

4. What cannot be returned for change of mind

  • Software, game codes, gift cards and licence keys once the code has been shown or the seal opened.
  • Items with a broken seal where the seal protects hygiene, such as in-ear earphones.
  • Custom PC builds and products ordered specially for you.
  • Items damaged after delivery.

These can still be returned if they are wrong, damaged on arrival, not as described or faulty.

5. Return delivery costs

  • Wrong, damaged on arrival or not as described: we pay the return delivery and refund the original delivery charge.
  • Change of mind: you bring the item to our store or pay the return delivery. The original delivery charge is not refunded.

6. Refunds

  • We check the returned item and tell you the outcome. If the return is accepted, we pay the refund within 7 days.
  • Refunds go back the way you paid where possible (the same wallet, bank account or card). For cash-on-delivery orders we refund by bank transfer, wallet or cash at the store, as you prefer.
  • Instead of a refund, you can choose a replacement or an exchange for another product (you pay or receive the difference in price).

7. Failed payments and cancelled orders

  • If money left your account but the payment failed or the order was not created, the payment service usually reverses it automatically. If it does not, we refund it within 7 days of finding out.
  • If we cancel your paid order (for example because the item is out of stock or we cannot deliver), or you cancel a paid order before it is sent, we refund the full amount within 7 days.

8. Faults after the return window

If an item develops a fault after 7 days, open a warranty claim from the same order page. See warranty information. If you are unhappy with how a return was handled, use the complaints page.
