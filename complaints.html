Complaints and privacy requests

If something went wrong with an order, a product, a payment or the way we handled your information, tell us here. We register it straight away, send you a reference number by SMS (and by email if you add your address) and give you a decision within 15 days.

Tell us what happened

We use these details only to handle your request (privacy policy). For a privacy request we may call you to confirm it is you before we share or delete anything.

What happens next

  • You get a reference number at once. Keep it for any follow-up.
  • We look into it and may contact you for more details.
  • We tell you our decision within 15 days.
  • If you are not satisfied with our decision, you can take your complaint to the Department of Commerce, Supplies and Consumer Protection, which handles complaints about online shops.
