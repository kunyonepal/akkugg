Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

At Akku.GG, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your data when you use our website and services.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to all information collected through our website www.akku.gg, mobile applications, and any related services (collectively, "Services").

By accessing or using our Services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Services.

Your Privacy Matters: We only collect information that is necessary to provide you with excellent service and comply with legal requirements. We never sell your personal information to third parties.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide to Us

We collect information that you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Create an account: Name, email address, phone number, password
  • Make a purchase: Billing and shipping address, payment information
  • Contact us: Name, email, phone number, message content
  • Leave a review: Name, rating, review text, product photos
  • Participate in surveys: Responses to survey questions
  • Enter promotions: Contact information and eligibility data

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you use our Services, we automatically collect:

  • Device information: IP address, browser type, device type, operating system
  • Usage data: Pages visited, time spent, links clicked, search queries
  • Location data: General geographic location based on IP address
  • Cookie data: Information stored in cookies and similar technologies

2.3 Information from Third Parties

We may receive information about you from:

  • Payment processors (transaction details)
  • Delivery partners (shipping and delivery status)
  • Social media platforms (if you connect your account)
  • Analytics providers (website usage statistics)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

3.1 To Provide Our Services

  • Process and fulfill your orders
  • Manage your account and provide customer support
  • Send order confirmations, shipping updates, and delivery notifications
  • Process payments and prevent fraudulent transactions
  • Respond to your inquiries and requests

3.2 To Improve Our Services

  • Analyze usage patterns to enhance user experience
  • Conduct research and development for new features
  • Test and troubleshoot technical issues
  • Gather feedback to improve product offerings

3.3 For Marketing and Communication

  • Send promotional emails about new products, special offers, and events
  • Display personalized advertisements and recommendations
  • Conduct surveys and market research
  • Send newsletters (only with your consent)
Email Preferences: You can opt out of marketing emails at any time by clicking the "Unsubscribe" link at the bottom of any promotional email.

3.4 For Legal and Security Purposes

  • Comply with legal obligations and regulations
  • Protect against fraud, abuse, and security threats
  • Enforce our terms and conditions
  • Respond to legal requests from authorities

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with:

4.1 Service Providers

We share information with third-party companies that help us operate our business:

  • Payment processors: To process transactions securely (eSewa, Khalti, banks)
  • Shipping partners: To deliver your orders
  • Cloud hosting providers: To store data securely
  • Email service providers: To send communications
  • Analytics providers: To understand usage patterns

4.2 Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred to the new owners.

4.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests from government authorities.

4.4 With Your Consent

We may share your information with other parties when you give us explicit permission to do so.

Third-Party Links: Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites. Please review their privacy policies.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption: All data transmission is encrypted using SSL/TLS technology
  • Secure storage: Personal data is stored on secure servers with restricted access
  • Access controls: Only authorized personnel can access your information
  • Regular audits: We conduct security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Payment security: We use PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Protect Your Account: Use a strong, unique password and enable two-factor authentication if available. Never share your password with anyone.

6. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience on our website.

6.1 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help us remember your preferences and improve website functionality.

6.2 Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential cookies: Required for basic website functionality (login, cart)
  • Performance cookies: Help us analyze how visitors use our site
  • Functionality cookies: Remember your preferences and settings
  • Advertising cookies: Used to display relevant ads based on your interests

6.3 Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling cookies may affect website functionality and your user experience.

6.4 Third-Party Cookies

We use third-party services like Google Analytics that may set their own cookies. These are governed by the respective third party's privacy policies.

7. Your Privacy Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

7.1 Access and Portability

  • Request a copy of your personal data
  • Download your data in a portable format

7.2 Correction and Update

  • Update or correct your account information
  • Request correction of inaccurate data

7.3 Deletion

  • Request deletion of your account and personal data
  • Note: Some data may be retained for legal or business purposes

7.4 Opt-Out

  • Unsubscribe from marketing emails
  • Disable personalized advertising
  • Opt out of data collection for analytics

7.5 Object to Processing

  • Object to the processing of your data for certain purposes
  • Restrict how we use your information
Exercise Your Rights: To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@akku.gg or through your account settings. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

Our Services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately. We will take steps to remove such information from our systems.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, regulatory, or operational reasons.

We will notify you of any material changes by:

  • Posting a notice on our website
  • Sending an email to your registered email address
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy

Your continued use of our Services after changes are made constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

Questions About Privacy?

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us:
