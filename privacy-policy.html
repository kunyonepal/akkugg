Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This page explains what personal information Akku.GG collects when you use akku.gg, why, who else receives it, how long we keep it and how to see, correct or delete it. We do not sell your personal information.

1. Who we are

This website is run by Akku.GG, Putalisadak, Kathmandu, Nepal.

For anything about your personal information, contact us at support@akku.gg, or use the complaints and privacy requests page.

2. What we collect and why

We only ask for what we need for the thing you are doing. Fields marked optional can be left empty.

When you place an order

  • Your name, mobile number and delivery address (and a landmark if you add one), to confirm the order by phone and deliver it.
  • Your email address, if you give it, to send the order confirmation and updates.
  • Company name and PAN, only if you ask for a business invoice.
  • What you ordered, prices, the payment method and payment status, to fulfil the order, issue the invoice, handle returns and warranty claims, and keep the accounts the tax law requires.
  • Online payments: the payment service tells us whether the payment went through, the amount and its reference number. We never see or store your wallet PIN, card number or bank password.
  • Your IP address and browser type, to spot fake or fraudulent orders.
  • How you reached us (for example an ad click or a link from another website), to know which of our ads work.

When you contact us or send a request

  • Contact form, PC build quotation requests, back-in-stock alerts, returns and warranty claims, complaints and privacy requests: the details you type in (name, phone, email, address for visits, your message), to answer you.
  • Product questions: the name you enter is shown publicly with the question and our answer. Your contact details are not shown.
  • Newsletter: your email address, only if you subscribe. Every newsletter has a link to unsubscribe.

When you browse

  • Search words typed into the site search, without who typed them, to see which products people look for.
  • Your browser's own storage keeps your cart, wishlist and similar choices on your device. The cookie policy lists them.

3. Who else gets it

We share personal information only with these services, and only what each one needs:

  • Payment services (eSewa, Khalti, ConnectIPS, Fonepay): when you pay with one of them, you enter your wallet or bank details on their page, not ours. They tell us whether the payment went through, the amount and their reference number.
  • Delivery riders and courier companies: your name, phone number, delivery address and the amount to collect, so they can deliver your order.
  • Sparrow SMS: sends our text messages about your order, return or question to your phone number.
  • Resend (an email delivery service): sends our emails about your order, return, password reset or question to your email address.
  • Telegram: our staff get alerts about new orders, messages and requests in a private Telegram group. Alerts include the name, phone number and what was ordered or asked.
  • Google Maps: the map on our contact and store pages loads from Google only when you click "Show map".
  • Our hosting provider: runs the server that stores the shop's data and encrypted backups.
  • Government bodies and courts: when the law requires it, for example the tax office (Inland Revenue Department) for invoices.

Some of these services are based outside Nepal, so the information they receive is stored on their servers abroad.

4. How long we keep it

  • Orders, invoices and payment records: 7 years, because the VAT law requires sales records to be kept for at least 6 years. After that the customer's name, phone, email and address are removed from them.
  • IP addresses, browser details and ad click ids stored with orders: 12 months.
  • Messages, quotation requests, bookings, back-in-stock alerts, complaints and privacy requests: 24 months after we receive them.
  • Copies of the texts and emails we send you: 90 days.
  • Newsletter: until you unsubscribe.
  • Backups: encrypted copies of the database are kept for 14 days, then deleted.

5. How we protect it

The website uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. Every staff member signs in with a password and a code from an authenticator app. Staff accounts have roles, and the admin pages each role can open are limited. Our nightly backups are encrypted. Online payment details are entered on the payment service's own page, not on ours.

No system is completely secure. If a security breach affects your personal information, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

6. Your choices and rights

  • See your information: anyone can ask for a copy through the privacy requests form.
  • Correct it: ask us to correct anything that is wrong.
  • Delete it: ask us to delete your information. We remove everything we do not have to keep by law; orders and invoices stay for the period in section 4.
  • Stop marketing: use the unsubscribe link in any newsletter.

We may need to confirm it is you (for example by calling the phone number on the order) before we share or delete anything. We reply within 15 days. If you are not happy with our answer, the complaints page explains where else you can go.

7. Under 18s

You must be 18 or older to create an account or place an order, or have permission from a parent or guardian, who is then responsible for the order. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18 without that permission. If you are a parent or guardian and think your child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we update the date at the top of this page. If a change affects how we use information we already hold, we will tell you on the website before it applies.
